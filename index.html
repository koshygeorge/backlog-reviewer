Gherkin Format Rules

  • Feature: Name of the feature being tested.
  • Scenario: A specific testable situation.
  • Given: Pre-condition / initial system state.
  • When: The action or trigger.
  • Then: Expected observable outcome.
  • And / But: Additional conditions or outcomes.
